Cookies and local storage

Last updated 26 October 2026. Stellar Split sets no cookies. It stores a few small items in your browser's local storage, and loads analytics only if you say yes.

Change your choice

You can change your mind at any time. Withdrawing analytics consent stops it on your next page load.

What is stored

All keys are in local storage and live on your device. Local storage is per website, so the landing site and the app each keep their own copy. None of these items is sent to advertisers.

KeyWherePurposeTypeDuration
stellar-split.consentLanding, docsRemembers your cookie choiceNecessary12 months, then we ask again
stellar-split.themeLanding, docs, appDark or light theme you pickedNecessaryUntil you clear it
stellar-split.accentAppAccent color you pickedNecessaryUntil you clear it
stellar-split.langAppLanguage you pickedNecessaryUntil you clear it
stellar-split.sessionAppKeeps you signed in. Holds the session token from your wallet sign-inNecessaryToken expires after 12 hours. Removed when you disconnect
stellar-split.disconnectedAppRemembers that you disconnected your walletNecessaryUntil you reconnect or clear it
stellar-split.demoAppRemembers that demo mode is onNecessaryUntil you switch it off or clear it
stellar-split.demo-stateAppSample data shown in demo modeNecessaryUntil you clear it
stellar-split.mock-stateApp (mock mode)Mock data when the app runs without the APINecessaryUntil you clear it
stellar-split.ratesAppCached exchange rates, so the app does not ask for them on every pageNecessaryRefreshed every few hours
Vercel Web AnalyticsLanding, docsCounts page views and referrers. Sets no cookie and stores nothing on your deviceAnalyticsOnly runs if you accept

Necessary items do not need consent because the site or app does not work as you asked without them. Analytics stays off until you accept it.

Other connections

When a page loads, your browser fetches fonts from Google Fonts. Google receives your IP address for that. We cannot load these fonts without that request. See the privacy policy for all providers.

Clearing it yourself

You can delete these items in your browser's site data settings at any time. The banner then asks again.
