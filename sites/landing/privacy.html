Privacy policy

Last updated 26 October 2026. Stellar Split is a hackathon prototype on Stellar Testnet. This page says what the code does today.

Who is responsible

The Stellar Split team is the data controller: five individuals who built the project at HackMeridian 2026 in Lisbon. There is no company behind it. For anything in this policy, use the contact in the last section.

What we collect

  • Your wallet address. The public Stellar address you sign in with. You prove control of it by signing a one-time message in Freighter. We never see your secret key.
  • Your profile. A display name and a home currency. An email address is optional and only stored if you type it in.
  • Group data. Group names and types, members (display name and address), invites, expenses (description, amount, who paid, how it is split) and settlements (amount and transaction hash).
  • Agent links. If you link a Stellar Assist agent, the owner address and the agent address.
  • Receipt photos. If you scan a receipt, the photo goes to Google's Gemini API to read the total and line items. Our API does not store or log the photo. It logs only that a scan happened, its size in bytes, how long it took and whether it worked.
  • Request logs. The API logs the method, path, status code and duration of each request. Our hosting provider also handles technical data such as IP addresses to deliver the site.
  • Browser storage. Small settings kept on your device. The full list is on the cookies page.

We do not ask for your name, address, ID or payment card. We do not sell data, show ads or build advertising profiles.

Public blockchain data

Payments you make settle on Stellar Testnet and are visible to anyone. They cannot be edited or deleted by us or anyone else. Your address and its payments are public by design. Testnet funds have no value, but the records are still permanent.

Transfers outside the EU

Several of these providers operate in the United States or other countries outside the European Economic Area. Where a transfer happens, it relies on the provider's own safeguards, such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or Standard Contractual Clauses. We have not verified in which region our Supabase project runs.

How long we keep it

There is no automatic deletion yet. Off-chain ledger data stays until you ask us to delete it. Sign-in sessions last 12 hours. The receipt photo is not kept by our API. Hosting and provider logs follow those providers' own retention. Data written to Stellar Testnet stays.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, delete, restrict or export your data, and to stop processing based on our legitimate interest. You can withdraw analytics consent at any time with the Cookie settings button in the footer, and withdrawing does not affect what happened before. To use a right, email us and name the wallet address you used so we can find your records. We answer within one month.

You can complain to the Spanish data protection authority, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (AEPD), at aepd.es, or to the authority in your own country.

Children

Stellar Split is not meant for anyone under 14. Do not use it if you are younger. If you think a child gave us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy we update the date at the top. For a change that affects analytics, we ask for your consent again.
